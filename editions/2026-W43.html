How this is made. Every Monday at 08:00 Lisbon time, Claude reads financial newsrooms (Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal, CNBC, Yahoo Finance), central bank releases (Federal Reserve, ECB, Bank of England, Bank of Japan), company results and SEC filings, and market data (US Treasury, CME FedWatch, EIA, World Gold Council, PitchBook), then rewrites this page. Each analysis lists its sources. Nothing here is investment advice.

Items marked (reported) come from press reports the company has not confirmed. Some figures reached us through daily news digests that summarize the primary report; those digests are listed as the source so you can trace them.
